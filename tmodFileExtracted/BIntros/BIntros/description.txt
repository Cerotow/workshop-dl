[b]This is a QOL (Quality of Life) RPG-like mod which Displays the Boss's title and subtitle with a nice typing and explosion animation and sound effects when spawning a boss.[/b]

Supports all bosses for the following:
[*] Vanilla
[*] Thorium
[*] Fargos's Souls
[*] Calamity
[*] Calamity Catalyst
[*] Calamity Noxus Boss
[*] Aequus
[*] Awful Garbage Mod
[*] Stars Above
[*] Consolaria
[*] Spirit Mod
[*] Polarities
[*] Spooky Mod
[*] The Depths
[*] Blue Sky Ore Mod
[*] Metroid
[*] Everglow
[*] Homeward Journey
[*] Secret of the Shadows
[*] Vitality

🛈 You can still play with unsupported mods but they won't have an intro.


Join our discord for simillar QOL mods: https://discord.gg/te6MPuWbtD
